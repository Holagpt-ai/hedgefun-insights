## Summary

- **Historical Episode Explorer (Batch 32):** Deterministic exploration of comparable momentum episodes from `RepeatMoverContext`, including session facts, match rationale split (known at episode vs observed afterward), linked catalyst evidence with causation disclaimer, post-momentum forward outcomes, and a scrubber over stored timeline or compact observation timestamps (no synthetic bars/prices).
- **Connected workflows:** Radar detail panel hosts the explorer; Historical Intelligence / Behavior link to `#historical-episode-explorer`; Repeat Movers opens the top comparable episode; Stock Detail links to Day Trade Radar via `?symbol=&focus=historical`.
- **Tests:** Pure module tests (chronology, future-timestamp rejection, rationale leakage) plus focused UI smoke test.
